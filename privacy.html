Privacy Policy for Lockout

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

Lockout is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Data We Collect and How We Use It

Lockout is designed as a "local-first" app. Your data stays on your device.

  • Fitness & Health Data: We store your workout logs, exercise sets, weights, body measurements, and progress photos. This data is used solely to provide you with tracking and analytics features within the App. It is stored on your device only — we have no servers and no copy of your data.
  • Purchase Information: We use Apple's StoreKit to manage subscriptions and tips. When you make a purchase, Apple verifies the transaction and shares your subscription status with us so we can unlock Pro features. We never see your name, payment card details, or billing information.

2. Advertising (AdMob)

Lockout shows banner advertisements to users on the free tier using Google AdMob.

  • To show ads, Google may collect and use device identifiers (such as the IDFA) to personalize your ad experience.
  • Upon your first launch, you will be asked for permission to "Track" via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. You can opt-out at any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • If you upgrade to Lockout Pro, all advertising and tracking related to AdMob are disabled.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • On-Device: Your workout data is stored locally in a secure database on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive or store any of it.
  • No Third-Party Sales: We never sell your fitness data, personal information, or workout history to third parties.
  • No Analytics Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics (like Google Analytics or Firebase) to track your behavior inside the App.

4. Your Rights and Control

  • Data Export: You can export all your data as JSON or CSV at any time from the in-app Backup screen.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all your data at any time by deleting the App from your device.
  • Permissions: You can revoke permissions for Notifications, Photo Library (for progress photos), or Tracking at any time via the iOS Settings app.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact Us
